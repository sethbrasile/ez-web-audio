<objective>
Add dispose event emission to BaseEffect (matching BaseSound pattern) and fix LFO to auto-clean up BaseEffect targets on dispose.

Purpose: Closes MOD-03 — LFO currently skips dispose cleanup for BaseEffect targets because BaseEffect doesn't emit 'dispose' events. This creates stale AudioParam references (memory leak). The fix has two parts: (1) make BaseEffect extend TypedEventEmitter and emit 'dispose' in dispose(), (2) remove the _isBaseSound guard in LFO._listenForDispose() so BaseEffect targets get the same auto-cleanup as BaseSound targets.

Output: BaseEffect emits dispose events; LFO cleans up all target types on dispose.
</objective>
