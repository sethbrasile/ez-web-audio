# Summary: Plan 54.2-01 — Effects & LFO Lifecycle Fixes

## What Was Built

Fixed three QC findings related to LFO and effect lifecycle management:

1. **QC-1-04 (High):** LFO `syncLifecycle` stop/end listener now calls `this.disconnect(target)` instead of `this.stop()`, so stopping one target only removes that target's connections. The LFO oscillator continues running for remaining connections and only stops when no connections remain.

2. **QC-1-13 (Medium):** Added `dispose()` overrides to CompressorEffect, DistortionEffect, EQEffect, and FilterEffect. Each disconnects its internal audio nodes before calling `super.dispose()`, following the established pattern from DelayEffect/ReverbEffect.

3. **QC-1-14 (Medium):** Documented the depth limitation in LFOConnectOptions JSDoc — when depthUnit is 'ratio' or 'cents', depth is calculated from the parameter value at connection time and won't auto-update. Also documented the BaseEffect subclass disposal contract.
