## Decisions Made
- ReverbEffect.dispose() disconnects comb.damping (BiquadFilterNode) in addition to comb.delay and comb.feedback — the plan listed `delay` and `gain` for the comb filter but the actual type has `delay`, `feedback`, and `damping`. All three must be disconnected for complete teardown.
- dispose() uses try/catch per-node (not a single catch block) to ensure maximum disconnection even if some nodes fail.
