Playhead
A single accent bar spans all six lanes, sweeping left→right; the current step's cells brighten and lift. Respects reduced-motion (bar jumps per step instead of easing).
Bar & beat legibility
32 steps split into 2 bars; every downbeat gets a numbered heavier divider, every beat a lighter one, so the eye finds "the one" instantly.
Share state
Share serializes the full machine (pattern, notes, BPM, swing, FX, lead) into the URL; button flips to a transient "Copied ✓" for ~2s, then restores.
